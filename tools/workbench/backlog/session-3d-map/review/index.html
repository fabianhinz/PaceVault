How to check

  1. Open a session with device laps (the demo "Passo San Giovanni loop" or the Lechlauf). The pill row shows the Map view pill (Globe, 2D) first, then Color by and Laps.
  2. Tap the 2D pill. A popover (or a drawer on the phone) offers 2D "Flat map" and 3D "Terrain from Mapterhorn". Pick 3D: the map tilts to 30°, centre and zoom stay, and the relief appears with a hillshade. The attribution (ⓘ, behind the panel) lists "© Mapterhorn" in 2D and 3D.
  3. Shift-drag (or Ctrl/Cmd-drag) on the map: left/right turns it around the cursor and up/down tilts it. On a phone, twist and drag with two fingers.
  4. Pick a lap by tapping the track, then step with the lap stepper. The lap is highlighted and framed at the current angle, and the rest of the track is dimmed. A click beside the track and a double-click do nothing.
  5. Hover the charts: the dot on the draped track jumps along with the cursor.
  6. Zoom out as far as you can: it stops at about two levels below the session fit, in 3D and in 2D.
  7. Pick 2D: pitch and bearing ease back to 0 and the zoom stays. Laps and lap framing look as before.
  8. Leave the session: the overview has no tilt and no zoom limit.
  9. Turn on flight mode and open a session you have not viewed in 3D. Pick 3D: the map tilts flat with the track on it.
  10. R1: in 3D, select a lap, then close it with ✕ or Esc: the whole track fills the map beside the panel (above the sheet on the phone), not a far zoom-out. Repeat after orbiting to about 60°.
  11. R2: in 3D, scrub a chart quickly on the phone: the dot and the hovered lap follow as smoothly as in 2D.
  12. R3: in 2D, Shift + left-drag on the map: no zoom rectangle appears.
  13. R4: select a lap: the Map view pill shrinks to its Globe icon, like Color by; close the lap and it shows 2D / 3D again.
  14. R5: on a long session (the Lechlauf), switch 2D → 3D and step through laps: the tilt starts without a noticeable hitch, lap switches are immediate.
  15. R6: in 2D, open the attribution: "© Mapterhorn" is listed.
  16. R7: switch 2D ↔ 3D several times and watch the track: it never disappears for a frame.
  17. R8: switch 2D → 3D → 2D a dozen times, quickly too: the 2D track is back every time, laps and the chart dot still work in 2D.
